## Domain Description

The system simulates a university study-space reservation platform that allows students to search for available rooms, view room details, make reservations, and manage bookings across campus buildings. A single server could become insufficient during high-demand periods such as midterms and final exams, when many students may search for rooms and submit reservations at the same time, creating risks such as slow responses, outages, or duplicate bookings. This system supports the common good by helping UMass students—especially commuter students, students living in crowded housing, and students without reliable access to a quiet workspace—find dependable places to study; when the system is unavailable or inaccurate, these students may lose access to limited campus study resources.
